REGEX FIND & REPLACE PRACTICE  (Sublime Text)
==============================================
HOW:  Ctrl+H opens Find/Replace. Turn ON the regex button  .*  (or Alt+R).
      Each task only affects ITS OWN block: select the block's lines first,
      then turn on "In selection" (the button next to .* , or Alt+S) before Replace All.
      Undo with Ctrl+Z if it goes wrong. Answers are in ANSWERS.md - try first!
      In Sublime's Replace box, groups are written $1 $2 ... (\1 \2 also works).
